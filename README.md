\# 🚨 ResQNet



\## AI-Powered Disaster Management \& Emergency Response Platform



ResQNet is an intelligent disaster management platform designed to help citizens, rescue teams, and authorities make faster and safer decisions before, during, and after natural disasters.



\### 🎯 Problem



During disasters such as floods, emergency responders often lack a unified view of:



\- Areas requiring immediate assistance

\- Blocked or unsafe roads

\- Available rescue shelters

\- People requesting emergency help

\- Areas likely to be affected next



\### 💡 Solution



ResQNet combines:



\- 🗺️ Interactive disaster maps

\- 🛰️ Satellite imagery

\- 🌧️ Weather and environmental data

\- 🤖 AI/ML-based risk prediction

\- 🆘 Emergency SOS

\- 🏠 Smart shelter recommendations

\- 🚧 Blocked-road detection

\- 🚑 Rescue-team prioritization

\- 🧭 Safe route planning
